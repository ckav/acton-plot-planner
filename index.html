What this is

A map per site with the plot numbers, paths, sheds, water and jobs. Tap a plot to see its card.

Moving around the map. Drag with one finger to move it, and pinch with two to zoom in or out (or use the + and - buttons). Fit shows the whole site again. On a long site, the buttons above the map jump to one part of it.

Nothing about plot holders lives on this page. The committee keeps that in its own record and looks it up by plot number. This demo shows where the details would sit, not the details.
